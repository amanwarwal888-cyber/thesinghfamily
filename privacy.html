Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This is the common Privacy Policy for the apps developed and published by Amandeep Singh ("we", "us", "the developer") for Android, iOS, and desktop, and for this website. It sets out the principles that apply to every app. Each app also has its own Privacy Policy that describes exactly what that app does with data. If the two ever differ, the app's own policy applies to that app.

Privacy Policies by App

Stock Management System

Fully offline. No account, no advertising, no data collected or sent anywhere.

 App policy

Future apps will be listed here when they are published, each with its own policy.

Our Principles for Every App

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.
  • Each app asks only for the permissions it needs for its stated features.
  • Each app's own policy states what data it handles, where that data is stored, whether anything leaves your device, and how you can delete it.

Information You Enter

Our apps are used to manage business or personal records that you enter yourself, which may include inventory and stock details, sales and billing records, purchase records and supplier quotations, and supplier contact and account details. This is data you choose to enter to run each app's features, and we do not use it for any other purpose.

This Website

This website is informational: it has no user accounts and no advertising. Please note the following:

  • The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • The pages load the Roboto font from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's font servers when a page loads.
  • The contact form on the Contact page does not store or send anything itself. It opens a pre-filled email in your own email app, and nothing is sent until you send it.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by our apps. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Each app's own policy explains the specific protections that apply to it.

Your Choices

You can review, edit, or delete the records you have entered directly within each app at any time. What happens to your data when you uninstall an app, and how to remove any exported or backed-up copies, is explained in that app's own policy.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are intended for business, administrative, and general personal use, and are not directed at children under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, or any app's own policy, from time to time. Any changes will be posted on the relevant page with a revised "last updated" date.
