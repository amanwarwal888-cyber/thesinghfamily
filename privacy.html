Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Stock Management System ("the app"), developed by Amandeep Singh ("we", "us", "the developer"), handles information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

The app is used to manage business records that you enter yourself, which may include:

  • Inventory and stock item details
  • Sales and billing records
  • Purchase records and supplier quotations
  • Supplier contact and account details

This information is business data you choose to enter for the purpose of running the app's features. We do not collect this data for any purpose beyond providing those features.

How Your Data Is Stored

The app may store inventory and business data locally on your device, in cloud storage, or a combination of both, depending on which features and settings you use. Where cloud storage is used, it exists to let you access or back up your own records, not to make your data available to third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Business and personal information entered into the app is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so while we work to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can review, edit, or delete the records you have entered directly within the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data from your device, except for any data you have separately backed up or synced to cloud storage.

Children's Privacy

Stock Management System is intended for business and administrative use and is not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
