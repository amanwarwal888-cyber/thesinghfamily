SM

Stock Management System

Inventory, Billing & Supplier Management

A complete, offline-first Android app that helps shops and canteens track stock, bill customers, and manage suppliers — all from one place.

Works offline Built for small businesses
Features

Everything your shop needs, in one app

Inventory

Track stock levels in real time, organize items by category, and get ahead of low-stock situations.

Sales

Generate bills quickly, record cash and loan sales, and keep a running ledger of every transaction.

Purchase

Log supplier purchases and quotations, and update stock automatically as new goods arrive.

Stock Reports

Review daily, weekly, and monthly reports to see what's moving, what's not, and where money is going.

Supplier Records

Keep a dedicated record for every supplier — orders, dues, and purchase history in one view.

About the developer

AS

Amandeep Singh

Stock Management System is built by Amandeep Singh, an independent Android developer focused on practical, offline-first tools for small businesses. The app grew out of everyday stock and billing needs and has evolved into a standalone product for shops and canteens looking to move off paper registers without depending on a constant internet connection.
